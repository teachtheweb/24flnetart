Net Art: Online Contemporary Art Practices
Exhibition of Fall 2024 Final Projects
Syllabus
Interactive Box Grid
By Ayobami Adegbite
I created this interactive grid project to explore how user interaction can transform visual elements dynamically. Each click changes the color, rotation, and reveals playful text, allowing users to engage and shape the composition in unique ways. For example, I used it to create a stickman, showcasing just one of the many creative possibilities this project offers.
A Colorful Quilt
By Antony Boguslavskiy
My project is a digital quilt with colors a noise. It displays the beauty of array of colors and noise mixed together.
Are You Tired?
By Tierney Robinson
This is a dialogue between the viewer and a funky HTML character that is sassy and beyond annoying at times. You can say it’s a bit stupid too, but, still fun to interact with. Vaguely inspired by the old "don't press the button" app, Are You Tired pushes the buttons of the user in more of a humorous way. Accompanied with the funky text are select meme’s and two videos which add more humor to this piece.
Pokemon: Hypertext Narrative Edition
By Adrian Cordova
My final is a Pokemon onboarding visual novel that remembers your input (name, mood) in order to have a dynamic message.
The Circus Crime
By Sophia Turcios
My project surrounds a murder mystery occurring in Gotham City that begins at the local circus. The project gives the users choices as the story goes along. You have to pick the right choices in order to not get fired from the job and to solve the mystery!
Ammon
By Dalia Fernandez
A web art adaptation of an excerpt from the short story Ammon by Maggie Siebert. I wanted to experiment with textures and images to emphasize the atmosphere of the narrative.
The A400
By Hisham Ettayebi
Based on the opening scene of Mission: Impossible - Rogue Nation, the IMF must stop a Russian Airbus 400M from taking off with WMDs on board.
A Day in New York City
By Sophia L Khodorovsky
In this hypertext narrative users will have the opportunity to play as a 25 year old living in a New York City apartment. Throughout the day they will choose where they go and what characters they interact with. The hypertext includes visual and audio elements to enhance user experience. How will your day go?
Pokemon Adventure
By Andrea Colon
Pokemon Adventure is about you, the Pokemon trainer, going out on an adventure, only to be met with an unexpected obstacle! Either run away, or, use your Pokemon to help you win the battle! But choose wisely, as the outcomes depend on your choices.
MEYLIN 𓆩♡𓆪
By Meylin Chicas
My project is a direct representation of myself. It encapsulates the multiple aesthetics I gravitate towards and identify with. I wanted my reworked desktop portrait to provide a better, more elaborate, insight into who I am. I pride myself on my style and this piece is a love letter to all the different fashions that make up my taste.
Campus Chronicles
By Helen Lee
My project is an interactive hypertext narrative that simulates the first day of college. This narrative allows users to navigate themes of friendship, romance, and adventure through choices that shape future outcomes. Inspired by the role-playing game Episode, my project combines storytelling and dynamic visuals to create an engaging experience and replicate the frenzy and limitless possibilities of freshman year.
Alien Planet Adventure
By Ayat Mughal
Alien Planet Adventure is an interactive web-based game designed to take players on a galactic journey. Players navigate through five mysterious planets, each locked behind riddles that challenge their knowledge of space. The game’s dark theme, captivating transitions, and fun surprise elements make it both visually appealing and engaging. The experience is crafted to entertain while subtly imparting interesting facts about our universe.
Day in the Life of a College Student
By Anne Tang
I created a simple simulator of a normal day in a college student's life. Choices such as eating breakfast, doing homework, or scrolling social media will affect the base stats motivation, health, grades, and social.
20th-Century Inventions That Changed the World
By Saumit Bose
This project explores the impact of four important 20th-century inventions: the airplane, the computer, the internet, and the nuclear reactor. By examining their development and subsequent cultural influence, this collection of pages aims to show how these innovations have changed the world.
Creative Space
Yamna Botello
This project is a dynamic, interactive web page that combines minimalist design with creative elements to encourage imagination and inspiration. By integrating light brown tones, subtle hover effects, and motivational quotes. I used these quotes because I think it represents me by being a positive person and also by being a person that tries to help others stay positive.
Blackpink
By Yunjie Chen
My project entails using HTML and CSS to build a digital tribute to Blackpink, one of the most significant girl groups in contemporary pop culture. The website includes images, a navigation menu, and responsive design. It utilizes text and images to share Blackpink's narrative and highlight their influence..
Formula 1 - 2024 Season
By Hiloni Patel
Formula 1 webpage offers an interactive experience where users can explore more about the 2024 season through hypertext narrative and linked HTML pages. On the homepage, four distinct cards respond to user interactions by transforming and leading to linked pages with additional information. The first card allows users to experience the day before the race itself, where 20 drivers battle to get the fastest lap to win the pole position and gain an advantage for the race itself. The remaining three cards allow users to learn about the current season's race schedule, constructor standings, and driver standings.
Reimagining Apple
By Jin Zhong
This project is a fresh take on the Apple product page, designed to be cleaner, more interactive, and visually engaging. I added smooth animations, hover effects, and easy links to Apple products to make the page more dynamic and user-friendly. It’s all about enhancing the experience while keeping the simplicity Apple is known for.
